# Lightning slot

A **lightning slot** is a name and a topic on the list for the 20:20 session. It
is a sign-up sheet on a wall, modelled honestly: **the order is the order people
signed up in**, and that is the order they speak in.

Anyone may add themselves. There is no cap, no approval, and no organiser
gate — a lightning list that needs approving is a lineup, and a lineup is a
different product. If more people sign up than there is time for, the room finds
out the way it always has: the organiser stops calling names.

Someone may **withdraw** their own slot before it starts, because signing up in
enthusiasm and thinking better of it ten minutes later is the single most
predictable thing that happens on a lightning list. They may withdraw only their
own — see [the attendee is a name in a browser](attendee.md).
