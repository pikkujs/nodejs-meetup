# Let the speaker see their own board

The speaker is talking, so they cannot read their phone, and they are facing away
from the projector. Everyone in the room can see what the room wants to ask them
except the person who will have to answer it.

A read-only `/app/stage` on a laptop turned toward the lectern would fix it with
no new code at all — which is an argument for a confidence monitor, not a
feature. Worth ten seconds of setup next time rather than a build.

Not built: nobody asked, and it may need no software.
