# Export the questions afterwards

At 21:40 the board holds every question the room wanted asked and the evening ran
out of time for — sorted, by definition, in order of how much the room cared.
That is the single most useful artefact the night produces, and right now it
exists only as rows in a SQLite file on a laptop.

A "copy all as markdown" button on the organiser screen would be about twenty
lines and would turn tonight into next month's talk lineup.

Not built: out of scope ("no past events"), and nobody asked.
